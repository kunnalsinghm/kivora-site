Autopilot

Govern and act, with a human in the loop.

Questions, answered.

How do we connect it?

Point your SDK’s base URL at the Kivora proxy, or send usage events to the ingest API. Provider support starts with OpenAI, Anthropic and Amazon Bedrock.

Will it change my model outputs?

Only if you approve it. Savings recommendations are cost-only estimates until quality is tested, and rollouts go 1%, 5%, 25%, 50%, 100% with rollback at every step.

Can it act without asking me?

You decide. Policies mark which actions need approval. The circuit breaker is the exception: it pauses a runaway agent immediately and logs why.
