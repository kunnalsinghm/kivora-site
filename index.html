      How do we connect Kivora?

      Point your SDK’s base URL at the Kivora proxy, or send usage events to the ingest API. Guard is built for OpenAI and Anthropic traffic and can also track self-hosted models.

      Will Kivora change anything without asking us?

      Guard enforces the budgets you set. When a team crosses its limit, it downgrades requests to a cheaper model you have approved, or blocks them if there is nothing cheaper, and says which rule did it. A key that behaves abnormally can be quarantined until someone reviews it. Nothing else changes on its own. For Autopilot, you choose which actions need approval, every action is logged, and reversible ones can be rolled back. The one planned exception is a circuit breaker that pauses a runaway agent immediately and logs why.

      When does Kivora launch, and what will it cost?

      Kivora is in pre-launch with no public launch date. Planned pricing is shown above and isn’t final. Request early access and we’ll contact you first.

      Is Kivora SOC 2 or otherwise certified?

      No. Kivora is pre-launch and doesn’t claim any compliance certification at this stage.

      Does Kivora cover cloud or Kubernetes cost?

      No. Kivora attributes AI request traffic by model, agent and team. It does not cover container or cloud-infrastructure billing.
