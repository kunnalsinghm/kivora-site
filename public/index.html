Frequently asked questions

How do we connect Kivora?

Point your SDK’s base URL at the Kivora proxy, or send usage events to the ingest API. Kivora Guard is built for OpenAI, Anthropic and Amazon Bedrock traffic and has been tested in simulation. Verifying it on live provider accounts is the next step.

Will it change our model outputs?

Only if you approve it. Savings recommendations are cost-only estimates until quality is tested, and rollouts move in stages with rollback at every step.

Can it act without asking us?

You decide. Policies mark which actions need approval. The circuit breaker is the exception: it pauses a runaway agent immediately and logs why.

When does Kivora launch, and what will it cost?

Kivora is in pre-launch and there is no public launch date yet. Planned pricing is shown on this page and isn’t final. Request early access and we’ll contact you first.

Can Kivora change routing without asking us?

Kivora can apply policy-approved changes, such as downgrading a model tier when a budget threshold is crossed. Each policy states whether a person must approve the action first.

Are changes reversible?

Executed actions are logged as evidence, and reversible ones can be rolled back. How a rollback works depends on the action type.

How are savings measured?

Verified savings are realised savings minus optimisation overhead, checked against the spend drop Kivora actually observed. Estimates that haven’t happened yet are shown separately as unrealised.

Is Kivora SOC 2 or otherwise certified?

Kivora is pre-launch and doesn’t claim any compliance certification at this stage.

Does Kivora cover cloud or Kubernetes cost?

No. Kivora attributes AI request traffic by model, agent and team. It does not cover container or cloud-infrastructure billing.

Who is Kivora for right now?

Engineering and platform teams running production AI workloads across more than one model provider, without a dedicated internal cost-allocation system yet.
