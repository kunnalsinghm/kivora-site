Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026. Zuvoxiae (“we”) runs this website for Kivora, which is in pre-launch.

What we collect

When you request early access we store the email address you enter, and any of these you choose to add: your company and a short note about what you want to fix. We also keep a one-way hash of your IP address (not the address itself), used only to limit spam.

Why

We use it only to contact you about Kivora access and to decide which teams to talk to first.

Where it is kept

In a hosted Postgres database provided by Neon. This site is hosted on Vercel, and when you submit the form we also receive an email notification at connect.kivora@gmail.com (a Gmail account). We do not sell your information.

How long

Until we have finished contacting people about early access, or until you ask us to delete it.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set cookies or run analytics. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends your IP address to Google to fetch them.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, write to connect.kivora@gmail.com.
